# 🏆 Medhat Bot: The All-in-One Fun & Economy Bot

Medhat Bot is built to make your server more active. It adds a fun money system, automatic rewards for active members, and a shop that's easy to manage.

---

## 👤 For Members
Here is what you can do with the bot:

*   **/bank**: Check your balance, claim your **Daily Coins**, and see your transaction history.
*   **/inventory**: See the items and name colors you own.
*   **/quest**: See your daily tasks and how much progress you've made.
*   **/trade**: Safely swap coins or items with other members.
*   **Easy Shop**: Click **[ Buy ]** buttons in the shop channel to get new roles and items.

---

## ⚙️ For Admins: `/settings`
The `/settings` menu is your main control panel. You can change everything about the bot from one simple screen.

### What you can manage:
*   **Money & Rewards**: Set how much members get for daily rewards and server boosting.
*   **Automatic MVP**: Automatically give the "Most Active" members roles and coins every day.
*   **Shop Setup**: Create items, put them in categories, and post them to your shop channels.
*   **Leaderboards**: Show who is the richest or most active member in any channel.
*   **Logs**: Send a record of everything that happens (like buys or trades) to your private admin channels.
*   **User Tools**: Easily give or take coins and items from any member.

---

## 💡 Good to Know
*   **Timezone**: The bot resets daily rewards and MVP winners at **Midnight Cairo Time**.
*   **Requirements**: Some shop items might need you to be a **Server Booster** or own another item first.
*   **Automatic Cleanup**: If you delete a role from your server, the bot automatically cleans up the shop so there are no "broken" items.
